Synclexia is a literacy support app designed specifically for children with dyslexia and reading difficulties. Combining evidence-based synthetic phonics with AI-driven personalization, Synclexia makes reading and writing practice engaging, accessible, and effective.

🎯 DYSLEXIA-FRIENDLY FEATURES:
• OpenDyslexic font + customizable letter spacing
• Color overlay options to reduce visual stress  
• Text-to-speech with word highlighting
• Adjustable font sizes (10-17pt)
• Phonics instruction based on synthetic phonics methodology

📚 LEARNING ACTIVITIES:
• Phonics Fun - Interactive letter and sound practice
• Reading Practice - Leveled stories with read-along support
• Letter Tracing - Guided handwriting with visual guides
• Spelling Challenges - Phonetically-organized word drills
• Phonological Awareness - Sound awareness and blending exercises
• Play & Learn Games - Engaging mini-games that reinforce learning
• Magic Scanner - Extract and read text from photos using OCR

🤖 AI-POWERED PERSONALIZATION:
• Analyzes performance to suggest appropriate difficulty levels
• Provides personalized learning insights
• Automatically adapts activities based on student progress
• Tracks learning data to identify patterns and trends

• Detailed activity logs and achievement tracking
• Educator content management and reporting
• Simple linking system for family connections
• Two-way feedback between parents and educators
