Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2, 2026

Synclexia is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users, especially children with dyslexia and learning differences. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use Synclexia, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, age, role (student, parent, teacher, or admin)
  • Profile Information: User preferences, accessibility settings, dyslexia-friendly font preferences
  • Parent-Child Linking: QR code data for linking parent and child accounts

1.2 Learning and Activity Data

To provide personalized learning experiences, we collect:

  • Activity Logs: Reading activities, phonological awareness exercises, spelling practice, and writing tasks
  • Progress Data: Performance metrics, completion rates, scores, and learning achievements
  • Adaptive Learning Data: AI-generated insights and personalized recommendations
  • Quest and Achievement Data: Gamification elements, badges, and progress tracking

1.3 Audio Data

Synclexia uses speech recognition features for interactive learning:

  • Microphone Access: Voice recordings for reading exercises and pronunciation practice
  • Speech Recognition: Audio data processed for educational feedback
  • Storage: Audio recordings are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored unless required for educational review

1.4 Camera and Media

  • Camera Access: Used for QR code scanning (parent-child account linking)
  • Image Uploads: Profile pictures and educational content uploads (teacher/admin features)
  • Document Picker: File selection for educational materials

1.5 Device and Technical Information

  • Device type and operating system (iOS/Android)
  • App version and usage analytics
  • IP address and general location data
  • Crash reports and error logs for improving app stability

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide Educational Services: Deliver personalized learning activities, adaptive content, and AI-driven insights
  • Track Progress: Monitor student learning progress and provide feedback to students, parents, and teachers
  • Improve Accessibility: Apply dyslexia-friendly fonts, spacing, and visual overlays based on user preferences
  • Parent Monitoring: Enable parents to view their child's activity logs and progress
  • Teacher Tools: Allow teachers to assign activities and monitor student performance
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
  • Communication: Send important updates, maintenance notifications, and educational tips
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Synclexia is designed for children with dyslexia and learning differences. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • Parental Consent: We require verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13
  • Limited Data Collection: We only collect information necessary for educational purposes
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We do not display ads or share children's data with advertisers
  • Parental Rights: Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information at any time
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track children across other websites or apps

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or your child's information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4.2 Limited Sharing

We may share information only in these circumstances:

  • Within the App: Student progress data is shared with linked parents and assigned teachers
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., Supabase for database hosting, Expo for app infrastructure)
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale (with continued privacy protection)

5. Third-Party Services

Synclexia uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services (see Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Expo: Mobile app development platform (see Expo Privacy Policy)
  • Speech Recognition: On-device speech processing (iOS Speech Recognition, Android Speech API)

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We ensure all third-party services comply with applicable privacy laws.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • Secure Authentication: Password-protected accounts with secure token-based authentication
  • Access Controls: Role-based access ensures students, parents, teachers, and admins only see appropriate data
  • Regular Backups: Data is backed up to prevent loss
  • Security Monitoring: We monitor for unauthorized access and security threats

Note: While we implement industry-standard security measures, no system is 100% secure. We continuously work to improve our security practices.

7. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: We retain data while your account is active and for educational continuity
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon account deletion, personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
  • Learning Records: Progress data may be retained in anonymized form for research and app improvement
  • Legal Obligations: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Access and Control

  • View Your Data: Access your profile and activity data through the app settings
  • Update Information: Modify your profile, preferences, and accessibility settings
  • Delete Account: Permanently delete your account and associated data
  • Export Data: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

8.2 Parental Rights (for Children Under 13)

  • Review all information collected from your child
  • Request deletion of your child's information
  • Refuse further collection or use of your child's information
  • Unlink parent-child accounts at any time

8.3 Communication Preferences

  • Opt-out of non-essential emails and notifications
  • Manage notification settings within the app

9. Permissions and Access

Synclexia requests the following device permissions:

  • Microphone: For speech recognition and pronunciation exercises (can be denied; affects speech features only)
  • Camera: For QR code scanning and profile pictures (can be denied; affects linking and profile features only)
  • Storage/Files: For uploading educational materials (teacher/admin features)

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Denying permissions may limit certain features but won't prevent basic app functionality.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Email to your registered address
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of Synclexia after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

11. International Users

Synclexia may be used internationally. By using the app, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy, regardless of your location.
